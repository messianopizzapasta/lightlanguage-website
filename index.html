What is Light Language?

Light Language uses coloured light and sacred geometry to make clear messages. Clear thought with clear intention radiates into the space around you — that is how the aura forms for the things you want to create and manifest in the world. It structures and transforms relationships, home, places, people, collectives, and patterns.

Three principles:

  1. Your thoughts create your reality.
  2. You are 100% responsible for your reality.
  3. As above, so below.

It comes down from the Maya and Aztec. Starr Fuentes learned it with Esperanza in Mexico. You sit with a teacher.

With thanks to Starr Fuentes and the Mayan healers, whose lineage carries this work and made these teachings available.

Grids

A Grid is a sequence of 7, 49 or 144 coloured geometries. A single shape you emanate is gone quickly. A Grid holds longer.

You start with Grids of seven shapes. Personal ones are for people and living beings, stationary ones for places, rooms and objects. Then dictionary Grids.

In the app you have Simple 7, Simple 12, 7×7 and 12×12. When you’re done, you export the Grid and can integrate it.
